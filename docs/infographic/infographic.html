Business problem

Defects, deferrals, work orders, component removals, shop findings and delays live in separate systems. Linking a write-up to the unit removed, the shop that repaired it and the flights it delayed takes hours, so rogue units and weak fixes are found late.

Pilot objectives

  1. Deliver a tangible reliability use case
  2. Build organizational knowledge and momentum
  3. One logical model for the graph and the lakehouse

Target use case: Fleet Reliability Explorer

“Enable reliability engineers and maintenance control to follow any defect to its aircraft, deferral, work order, removed unit, repair shop and operational impact, through a unified ontology.”

Success criteria

  • Validated concepts and relationships
  • 10 competency questions answered
  • Graph and Delta tables from one ontology
  • Stakeholder demonstration
  • Clear path for expansion
